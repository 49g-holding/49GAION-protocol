# 49GAION Operating Protocol v1.6.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decisions: twenty records of the hub, [`DR-0008`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0008-source-lifecycle-first-stage.md) to [`DR-0027`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0027-operations-event-register.md), each listed with its subject in [`decisions/README.md`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/README.md). The rows of `CHANGELOG.md` marked `v1.6.0` give each change in full.

## Summary

### Work between units

- Mechanisms from practice (DR-0010):
  - Work Item optional fields and the `decision-request` type;
  - coordination log and leases; loop breaker, repair tripwire and dispute exit; honest exit;
  - the receivability check, zero-cost wake and the delegation ladder;
  - §14 Records and §15 Halt, pause and cut-off at work.
- Position Principle at work: position codes only; occupancy and handover in the register; occupants resolved at action time; vacant positions (DR-0011, DR-0012, §4.10 items 16 to 21).
- Two positions are two parties: the signature tests count distinct positions, never persons (DR-0015).
- Engines at the tool gateway: the engine register, and the engine bound and the engine that served each call, in the gateway log (DR-0020).

### The Library

- Source Lifecycle, the first stage (§13; DR-0008, DR-0009).
- Process and Position Design (§17; DR-0014).
- Product Core, the organisation's one product division (§18).
- Target Operating Model (§19; DR-0016).
- Delivery and Implementation (§20; DR-0017).
- The design chain connected (§21; DR-0018, DR-0019, DR-0023), with:
  - the design return contract and the release notice contract;
  - Source Lifecycle serving the design stages;
  - TEST-DESIGN-CHAIN v1.
- The Library register:
  - in two sections, Parts and Products, with the assemblies Organisation Design and Delivery and Organisation Memory (DR-0025, DR-0027);
  - mother blueprints pinned in their own public mother repositories (DR-0024);
  - the reference-model catalogue `v0.1.0` (DR-0022);
  - Engine Room named (DR-0026).
- Operations Event Register, the record of the live operation (§22; DR-0027).

## Checks on main

card-schema-test also runs the Library contracts check: the design return and release notice contracts against their valid and must-fail examples, and the reference-model catalogue against its schema.

## Known limits

- The Library is written as a map first. Every part card is `DRAFT`, and its fixed test reads `NOT_RUN` until the part is built and run.
- Engine Room has no card, fixed test or mother blueprint yet (DR-0026, item 4).
