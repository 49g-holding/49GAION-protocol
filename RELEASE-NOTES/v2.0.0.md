# 49GAION Operating Protocol v2.0.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decisions: the hub's records [`DR-0044`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0044-three-mothers-in-repositories-of-49gaion.md) to [`DR-0047`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0047-the-clean-version.md). Released on the governing seat's words of 26 October 2026 (translated): "my confirmation was for all your proposals", the clean version among them.

## Summary

### The clean version (`49GAION/DR-0047`)

- **The Operating Protocol, the Library register page, the contracts page and the reference-model catalogue are written clean.** Every Addition is folded into the body; every Reading has replaced the sentence it read; the wording that was superseded is listed once, verbatim, at the end of each text under "Superseded wording, by version". Each section that folded anything ends with a line naming the records it drew on.
- **No rule, stage, port, schema, pin or card changes.** The schemas under `spec/schemas/`, the Library register's pins and hashes, the part cards and the fixed tests are those of `v1.8.0` with the changes of the rows marked `v1.9.0` in the changelog (the register re-pinned to the mother repositories, `49GAION/DR-0044`; the licence texts, `49GAION/DR-0045`; the readings and the optional `original_requester` of the review of 26 October 2026).
- **From now on** each release of the Protocol is written clean, like the hub's texts.

### The mothers in repositories of 49GAION (`49GAION/DR-0044`)

- The Library register pins Target Operating Model, Process and Position Design and Delivery and Implementation at their own mother repositories, `49GAION-<part>`; the instance repositories they came from are archived with a pointer.

### The owner and the licence (`49GAION/DR-0045`, `DR-0046`)

- `LICENSE.md`, `LICENSE` and `LICENSES/` as in the hub.

## Companions

Released with this version: the hub's Standard `v2.0.0` (`49g-holding/49GAION`), written clean, and the AIOC definition and blueprint template `v2.0.0` (`49g-holding/49GAION-aioc`). The mother repositories keep their own versions and move their Protocol pin to this release by their own pull requests.

## Known limits

- **Nothing has run yet.** Every part card is `DRAFT`, and every fixed test of every part reads `NOT_RUN` until the part is built and run. Edition Maker is specified, not built.
- **The Library's tests are prose** except where a part has executable checks; executable must-fail tests for the authority ceiling (tests 52 and 53) follow in their own change.
