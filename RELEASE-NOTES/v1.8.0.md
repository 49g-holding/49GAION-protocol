# 49GAION Operating Protocol v1.8.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decisions: the hub's records [`DR-0032`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0032-withdrawn-aion-builder-stages-delivery-test.md) to [`DR-0043`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0043-the-49gaion-guide-version-1.md), each listed with its subject in the hub's [`decisions/README.md`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/README.md). Released with the hub's Standard `v1.8.0`, on the governing seat's word of 2026-10-25 (translated): "I agree with all of it. Do all of it."

## Summary

### Withdrawn

- **The AION Builder, its six stages, Ionize, the delivery test and the two tests at v3** (`49GAION/DR-0032`). The `org-aion-record` schema drops `stages` and `delivery_test`; the AION Builder's cards leave `library/cards/`; the lines of the Protocol and of the reference organisations' checks that cited them are rewritten to the rules that stay in force: the GOV seat, the Human Floor, the stop door and the accountability chain.
- **49GAION Target AI Operation** (`49GAION/DR-0034`): named in the Library register on 22 October 2026 and withdrawn the same day; its row has left the register.

### Edition Maker and the `[ORG]AION`

- **The Library register names 49GAION Edition Maker** as 49GAION's own start point: it processes every order or request made to 49GAION through its own intake stages, takes in what an organisation is for, and gives back the first core of its `[ORG]AION`, a first AIOC at the level of the governing seat, with three stages of work (`49GAION/DR-0035`, `DR-0038`).
- **One name,** `[ORG]AION`, throughout (`49GAION/DR-0036`).
- **Live.** Each `[ORG]AION` configures a part inside the organisation, through its own live design; a Library part is a starting point, never a finished system (`49GAION/DR-0037`).
- **Edition Maker's requirements live here,** in `library/edition-maker/`: the PRD, its fixed test, the reasons, the raw reviews and a light trial, generalised (`49GAION/DR-0039`). The PRD is at v0.9: v0.8 gave the core's instruction its first named capability, Structural Adaptation (`49GAION/DR-0040`); v0.9 brought it into line with the complete definition and cut nothing (`49GAION/DR-0041`); the header records the governing seat's confirmation of its approval and the fresh keys at the handover (`49GAION/DR-0042`).

### The complete definition

- **Readings of `49GAION/DR-0041`** at the head of the Protocol and in the Library register: where the Protocol differs from the complete definition, the definition governs until the Protocol is rewritten at its next version. The README points to the definition.
- **Readings of `49GAION/DR-0042`:**
  - at the head: where the Protocol says that something always holds, it holds in every edition 49GAION hands over;
  - §4.1, the Work Item: every item carries its original requester, the sender of the first item of its chain; an act beyond the original requester's authority is refused at the gateway, however it is routed;
  - §4.9, the signature gate: a fresh live sign-in for each signature; a standing approval covers only changes that can be undone and do not widen access, budget or authority; a governance seat with its own conflict of interest, signing alone, is refused;
  - §23, the Charter: the gateway and the stop run as code beside the core; any change to them counts as loosening; fresh keys are issued at the handover.
