# 49GAION Operating Protocol v2.1.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decisions: the hub's records [`DR-0048`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0048-only-aiocs-and-hoac-positions.md) and [`DR-0049`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0049-positions-only-the-texts-brought-into-line.md). Released on the governing seat's word of 28 October 2026 (translated): "As long as any work of 49GAION remains, we do not go to [the organisation's AION]. Go and set all those works right too."

## Summary

### Positions only (`49GAION/DR-0048`, `DR-0049`)

- **Two kinds of actor.** The Protocol knows only AIOCs and HOAC positions. Who holds a position, and how, is the organisation's own mechanism, within the law and outside the system.
- **Withdrawn:** the arrangement models Solo and Team; the register entries `arrangement`, `occupancy` and `handover`, and occupant and person IDs; the acting filler of a human Cell; deputies of human seats and signature referral. Their former wording is listed verbatim at the end of the Protocol.
- **Kept, by position:** the Overseen link (set below Top Management by Top Management, and on Top Management by the governance seat; an empty overseer passes the held action to the position above, which may clear or stop it); a personal signature with no answer stays held and is raised along the accountability path, and no other seat signs in its place; a signature is verified by the credential the organisation's mechanism issued for the position and a fresh live sign-in, and stamped with the position code and time, with no reference to a person.
- **Schemas.** `unit-card` withdraws `decision_rule`, needs a `label_holder` for a HOAC label, and gives a human Cell no `filler`. `signature` withdraws `signer_identity_ref`, `signer_role`, `delegation_ref` and `bound_to_one_person`. New rules: RULE-DECISION-RULE-WITHDRAWN, RULE-NO-HUMAN-FILLER, RULE-NO-PERSON-REFERENCE, RULE-NO-ACTING-OR-DEPUTY.
- **Fixtures.** No person IDs in the reference organisations; `three-position-company` and `gov-and-tm-only` in place of the former person-named organisations; five new must-fail fixtures; the superseded fixtures kept under `superseded/`.
- **Edition Maker.** Its requirements, v0.10, read the same way (section 15).
