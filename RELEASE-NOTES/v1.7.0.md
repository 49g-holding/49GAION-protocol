# 49GAION Operating Protocol v1.7.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decisions: four records of the hub, [`DR-0028`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0028-chief-of-staff-written-cos.md) to [`DR-0031`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0031-ai-native-the-definition-and-the-introduction.md), each listed with its subject in the hub's [`decisions/README.md`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/README.md). Released with the hub's 49GAION Standard `v1.7.0`.

## Summary

- **The Chief of Staff written COS.** The unit card's `processes[].served_by` accepts every Cell code, so that a Chief of Staff Cell's code under Naming Standard R16 is accepted (DR-0028).
- **The Charter** (§23; DR-0029), the root of every `[ORG]AION`. §23 sets:
  - its shared services, the Gateway, Records and Work Pods, one of each;
  - that no AI holds the keys;
  - the order of starting;
  - that nothing blocks the accountability path.

  It comes with the Library card `charter.part-card.json` v1 and TEST-CHARTER v1.
- **Five readings from the Charter mother; on the Protocol side** (DR-0030):
  - `hoac_over` returns the GOV Cell for a change, installation or retirement of a Pod or Cell of the Charter (§4.10, item 11);
  - a reading of `enforcement` in the port card schema;
  - a reference-organisation query.
- **The Library register.** Seven mother blueprints are pinned at their merged commits: Delivery and Implementation at v0.2.1 and the Charter mother at v0.1.1.
- **AI-native** (DR-0031). §17 item 7 reads the later stage, for an `[ORG]AION`, as the AI-native design. The register reads the next product as AI-native, not simply a choice of which positions an AI fills.
