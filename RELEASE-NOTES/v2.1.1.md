# 49GAION Operating Protocol v2.1.1

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decision: the hub's record [`DR-0051`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0051-two-reviews-of-v2-1-0-corrected.md). Two reviews of `v2.1.0` were checked against `main` and corrected in one round, on the governing seat's word of 29 October 2026 (translated from Persian): "build it all at once; combine both and go through one correction round."

## Summary

- **Overseen (§4.10, item 18).** An irreversible action (a sent message, a payment, a deletion) runs only on the overseeing position's clearance; a lapsed hold window never releases it, and the lapse is raised. A reversible action keeps the lapse rule.
- **The Chief of Staff (§4.10, item 6; §10, item 3; §13).** A Chief of Staff, or an attached Cell, signs for its position only within the grant that position has given it, tagged as a delegated act; no bot signs a listed action.
- **The complete definition, where the text had missed it:** the gateway and the stop run as code beside the core, outside the reach of every AI (§10, item 6; §13); §4.2 and §20 by position; "a party other than" (§23).
- **Edition Maker's requirements, v0.11:** three engines at every step, and a step waits with fewer; the smallest edition by position; every request becomes the organisation's own `[ORG]AION`.
- **The Library register** re-pins the seven AIOC part mothers at their releases of 29 October 2026, which carry `49GAION/DR-0049` (positions only).
- **The checks:** `no-persian` scans every Arabic-script block in file contents and names, the pull request and its new commits; `no-org-names` matches written variants; `ref-check` follows relative links; the Library's shared checks gain `links`, and their `pin` check fails on a Protocol commit no release tag names.
- The wording of the rules that was superseded is listed at the end of each text, verbatim, under `v2.1.1`; corrections of a link, a label or a version are not listed.
