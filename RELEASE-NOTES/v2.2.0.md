# 49GAION Operating Protocol v2.2.0

Approved by the governing seat (GOV) of 49GAION. Decision: the hub's record [`DR-0053`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION/blob/main/decisions/DR-0053-the-review-rooms-designs-v2-2-0.md), on the governing seat's word of 29 October 2026 (translated from Persian): "This new work they proposed: if you agree, and it is right, and it contradicts what I want nowhere, do the real request."

## Summary

- **One event record (§24).** Every event at the gateway is one entry of an append-only record, chained by hash, in one envelope: `spec/schemas/event.schema.json`, a profile of the OpenTelemetry semantic conventions for generative AI, with 49GAION's fields under `gaion`. Each decision names its rule, the version in force by tag and commit, and the record that authorises it; an act done for another position stays within the permissions common to its whole chain.
- **Tainted runs (§24, item 5).** After one external read, a send, a publication, a write outside the part's space, a new target or a change of permission runs only after a recorded clearance; stored content keeps its label.
- **The conformance scenarios (§24, items 7 and 8).** Traces that must pass and traces that must fail, each on one rule, under `tests/traces/`, with `check_traces.py`. They test the checker; an implementation conforms when its own gateway's traces of the same scenarios pass.
- **A new credential is an event (§4.10, item 22),** reported at once; an optional waiting period before it signs a listed type; a stop is never delayed.
- **Signatures (§4.9, items 10 and 11).** The floor and quorum of a type; a signature bound to its subject, single-use, within its validity, with signer and executor distinct; five new rules in the test of the forms.
- **The Chief of Staff (§4.10, items 6 and 7; §10, item 3; §24, item 9),** as the governing seat ruled on 29 October 2026: a human position works with AI through its own Chief of Staff and may give it any reach, up to full access and signing for it, a listed action included; whatever the Chief of Staff does under the grant is the position's own decision. A position grants only to its own Chief of Staff; a Chief of Staff gives access to another AI part only where its grant lets it. The grant is an event of the record; the trace checker and the signature record follow it.
- **Informative (§25):** the gateway on tool calls through the Model Context Protocol and on calls between agents.
- **The checks:** the test of the forms runs the traces; `pin-lag` reports each Library pin against the latest release; the Library's shared checks gain `dr-source`.
