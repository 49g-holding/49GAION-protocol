- **Date:** 2026-10-11

- **Status:** in force

## Decision

1. **The framework.** 49GAION is an organisation design framework: a framework for building organisations from interchangeable AI and human parts. It consists of the governing seat (GOV), the 49GAION Standard, the 49GAION Protocol, the 49GAION Library, 49GAION Self-Improvement and the AION Builder. To the outside world it appears only as the words "Powered by 49GAION".
2. **The product.** 49GAION's product is `[ORG]AION`, the AI Operating Nexus of one organisation, shown as "`<Brand>` AION, Powered by 49GAION". Each `[ORG]AION` is built for one organisation, has its own `[ORG]HOAC-GOV` from day one, stands on its own Human Floor and, once delivered, belongs entirely to the customer.
3. **The starting point.** v1.0.0 is the starting point of 49GAION. The Standard, the Operating Protocol, the AIOC definition and blueprint template, the Naming Standard and the HOAC register on `main` of `49GAION`, `49GAION-protocol` and `49GAION-aioc` are in force as written.
4. **Names.** The only names are 49GAION (the framework) and `[ORG]AION` (the product).
