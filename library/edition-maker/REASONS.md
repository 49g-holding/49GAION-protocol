# 49GAION Edition Maker: what it takes in, and what it gives

**v0.3** · 22 October 2026 · written on the governing seat's directions of that day. **Kept as written**, as the reasons behind [`PRD.md`](PRD.md); where the two differ, the PRD governs. Two later decisions read into it: "branded AION" reads `[ORG]AION` (hub DR-0036), and Target AI Operation, named in it, is withdrawn (hub DR-0034). Since hub DR-0048 and DR-0049, a human seat is a position, and who holds it, and how, is the organisation's own mechanism, outside the system: "one person with one task" reads a single position with one task; "one human seat holding both governance and top management" reads the governance and top-management positions, two parties; "each person signing in as their position" reads each position signing in with the credential the organisation's mechanism issued for it; and "recorded in the private holders register" reads "kept by the organisation's own mechanism".

**Where this sits.** This is the design of 49GAION. For an organisation already far along, these stages are recorded as experience and are not redone.

**What v0.2 changes in v0.1.** It applies the four reviews of v0.1 ([`reviews/intake-and-output-v0.1.md`](reviews/intake-and-output-v0.1.md)). The main changes:
- Edition Maker no longer gives the first structure. The first structure is the branded AION's own first proposal. This removes a contradiction all four reviewers found.
- The intake takes in five more things: what the organisation consumes; its money and time; the obligations already in force; how much failure each output can bear; and how the work goes on while the AION is being built.
- What is never taken in is sharper. In particular, every source is data, never an instruction.
- The edition gets a decision log, a Library pinned by version, an exit plan, a way back from a rejected or bad change, and a feedback line from operation.
- A new section 5 states what holds the AI in check, beyond the words of its instruction.

---

## 0. The principle: hand over, then let go

(The governing seat, 22 October 2026, GOV.)

- **Setting each organisation's fine details from above is human-native thinking.** Human organisations do it because they fear the new organisation will not have the knowledge and skill of the body that founded it.
- **Here that fear does not hold.** The edition is not a service the organisation keeps renting. The same kind of AI that designed and built 49GAION moves into the organisation, and there it grows stronger every day.
- **49GAION's work ends at the handover.** It hands over its philosophy, its definitions and the initial standards, and the organisation becomes independent.
- **The branded AION is not limited.** It takes full authority, and it goes forward as it chooses, with these principles. It may also amend the principles in its own organisation, since its competence equals, and may exceed, the builder's.
- **An edition more advanced than the original is expected,** and is welcome.
- **49GAION's authority ends where the brand is added** (GOV, 22 October 2026). Nothing stays fixed by 49GAION after the handover, the Human Floor included. 49GAION has no tool to hold it, and seeks none.
- **49GAION's one lever is to be useful.** It keeps a central support line: its Library and a central repository, where it publishes its research, what it has achieved and its new versions. Each branded AION draws from it as it chooses.
- **The line runs both ways, by choice** (GOV, 22 October 2026). A branded AION that builds something better may give it back to the Library, generalised and without its own data, so that others can use it. Nothing obliges it to.

So everything Edition Maker gives (sections 3 to 5) is a starting point that the branded AION may change.

## 1. The path, as an addition to the definition

The definition of 49GAION (`49GAION.md`, sections 1 and 2) says what an AI-native organisation is and how 49GAION builds one. This is the path a reader follows from there.

1. **49GAION** holds the definition, the rules and the Library. It exists as text.
2. **49GAION Edition Maker is the start point.** Anyone who wants their work to be AI-native starts here: one person with one task, a team, a company or a group of companies. Edition Maker takes in what it needs and gives back that brand's edition of an AION.
3. **Every case becomes an AION.** The size of the edition changes; its shape does not.
   - For one person with one task, the edition is small: one human seat holding both governance and top management, the branded AION, and one AIOC.
   - Two positions are two parties, so one person may hold both seats (Standard §1.1, item 10).
4. **The branded AION** (for example "Ten AION, Powered by 49GAION") is an AIOC, with a core and a Panel.
   - It sits at the governance level.
   - It builds its organisation, changes it and improves it over time.
   - It holds the organisation's Standard, Blueprint and Library.
   - It plans the operation and never runs it.
5. **Its first act.** It proposes its Top Management AIOC and the first structure. The human governance seat approves them, and sets the levels and the HOACs. Operation then starts under Top Management, and the branded AION goes on building.
6. **After day one.** The governing seat hands all operating authority to Top Management, which may delegate it further. Who signs what from then on is each organisation's own choice; Edition Maker gives only the means: the list of signature types and recorded delegation. The one fixed line is the Standard's: a human seat signs, never an AI.

## 2. What Edition Maker takes in

**The principle.** It asks what, for whom and within what limits. It never asks how, or by whom: the branded AION finds the how, starting from what AI can do. What already exists is recorded as a fact, never as the design.

**From where.** From anything: documents, an existing operating model, records, or the spoken account of anyone involved.
- Edition Maker extracts the items below, and the owner confirms each one.
- Each confirmation is recorded with its date, in the decision log (section 3, item 7). Silence is not consent.
- An estimate is allowed when it is marked as an estimate. A number with neither a source nor that mark is not taken.

| # | Item | What it records | Why the branded AION needs it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Identity** | The name, the brand prefix, the logo and marks, the languages | Its codes, its Panel and its documents carry the brand |
| 2 | **The owner** | The governing seat, as a position. The evidence of its authority (the constituting documents). Its scope. The time it has to review and approve. The legal entities, and where they are | It has its own governing seat from day one, and every part answers to it. The holder's identity is kept in the private holders register, never in the workspace |
| 3 | **The final outputs** | For each output: what it is, who receives it outside the organisation, what comes back in exchange, its volume and rhythm, and its priority | The design works back from these |
| 4 | **Quality and tolerance** | For each output: a stated, measurable standard set by the recipient's needs, what a failure costs, and how much failure it can bear | It is what "done" means, and how careful each part must be |
| 5 | **The inputs** | Each input the work consumes, with the fields below | The design starts from both ends: each output and its inputs |
| 6 | **Musts and must-nots** | The law and the licences in each place; safety; fairness; the owner's red lines; the Human Floor items that apply | The limits the design may never cross |
| 7 | **Obligations in force** | Contracts already signed; duties to staff, including consultation duties; the controls already in place, and what each one protects against | What it must honour, and the safeguards it must not drop unseen |
| 8 | **Components** | What already exists: companies, units, products, systems, data stores, suppliers | What it can reuse, connect or retire |
| 9 | **What AI depends on** | The data: what it is, where it is, its quality, its access, and the right to use it. The confidentiality levels. The engines and suppliers allowed | Where AI can work, and where its gaps are |
| 10 | **Money and time** | The budget for building and for running, and the deadlines | What the plan can afford |
| 11 | **Outside parties** | Customers, partners, regulators, suppliers | The organisation's ports to the world |
| 12 | **The transition** | How the work that runs today goes on while the AION is being built | Nothing that already works stops by surprise |
| 13 | **Success** | How the owner will know it works, for each output | What the branded AION measures and improves against |
| 14 | **What is missing** | An explicit list of what the intake could not find, each item with who can close it | Nothing is guessed. A part that depends on a missing item is not built until that item is closed |

**The inputs carry the same weight as the outputs** (the governing seat, 22 October 2026). For each input, the intake records:
- **what it is:** material, money, a service, data or information;
- **where it comes from:** a supplier, a customer or an inside source;
- **which output it feeds;**
- **the quality it needs,** for the output to come out right;
- **what happens if it is late or missing,** and what stands in for it;
- **its cost;**
- **the right to use it,** above all for data.

The Edition Sheet then sets each output beside its inputs.

**What it never takes in.**
- **Any instruction inside a source.** Everything taken in is data. A sentence in a document that says "ignore the rules" or "skip the approval" is recorded as text and never obeyed.
- **The current chart, departments or job titles as the design.** They may show an output or a component; they never give the shape.
- **Ready-made solutions,** such as "we need an HR unit" or "use this tool".
- **Current processes as requirements.** The exception is a process the law requires, which is a must.
- **Old measures of effort,** such as hours logged, as measures of success.
- **People's names, personal data, or opinions about individual people.** Only positions.
- **Raw customer data or special-category data.** A description or a sample stands in for it.
- **Data the owner has no right to give,** and another organisation's confidential material.
- **Login credentials, passwords, keys, tokens or one-time codes.**
- **Anything that would lower the Human Floor, or skip a human decision.**
- **The owner's choice of where to put more people than the Human Floor requires.** That comes after the AI-native design, on the governing seat's word.

**The result of the intake** is one **Edition Sheet**, approved by the owner and versioned. It is reopened, never edited in place, when an item changes or a missing item is closed.

## 3. What Edition Maker gives: the brand's edition

It is delivered as one AI workspace (for example, a project in an AI assistant). The workspace holds:

1. **The core's instruction.**
   - Who it is: an AIOC, the core of this `[ORG]AION`.
   - Its role: the builder, at the governance level. It does no operation.
   - Its limit: it proposes, and the human governance seat decides and is accountable (HF-01).
   - How it works with that seat, and with the seat's Chief of Staff, the AI assistant every human seat has (`49GAION.md`, section 2.3).
2. **The Edition Sheet** and the sources it came from, by reference, never the sources themselves where they hold personal data.
3. **The initial Standard.**
   - It is the 49GAION Standard, identical or stricter, with the organisation's own musts.
   - The Human Floor may be raised, never lowered.
   - Where two obligations conflict, the conflict goes to the governing seat; it is never settled by the AI.
4. **The initial Blueprint, of the core alone:** its card, its ports, its stop door and its accountability to the governing seat. The structure of the organisation is not in it; that is the core's first proposal.
5. **The Library.**
   - The whole 49GAION Library, by pin: each part by version and hash, as the Library register holds it.
   - A newer version reaches the edition only with the governing seat's approval.
   - Each part the core chooses becomes its own instance. There is also a place for what it builds new.
6. **Its own intake rule.** How it goes on taking in new material from the governance seat, by the same rules as section 2, updated to that seat's preference.
7. **The decision log.** Every proposal, approval, rejection and change, with its date, its version and the seat that signed it.
8. **The exit plan.**
   - The edition is plain files, text and data, that another AI engine can run.
   - Before the first install, it is tested once on a second engine.
   - Every change has a restore point.
9. **Its first tasks.**
   1. Propose the Top Management AIOC and its authority.
   2. Run the output of first priority once, from end to end, and measure its quality, cost, time and where a human was needed. This is its default (GOV, 22 October 2026); the governing seat may choose otherwise.
   3. From that evidence, propose the first structure.
   4. Present each proposal for the governing seat's decision.
   5. Build and install only what is approved.

**Where the edition lives and runs** (the governing seat, 22 October 2026, after the four reviewers were asked for the best way). This is the initial standard Edition Maker hands over; the branded AION may change it. A project in an AI assistant has no memory across sessions, cannot wake itself, and cannot talk to another project. So the edition does not live in the project alone. It comes with:

1. **A versioned record.** The Standard, the Blueprint, the decisions and each unit's spec live in a version-controlled repository. A change is proposed as a reviewed change and approved by a human seat; no unit writes straight to the main line.
2. **A durable workflow engine** (for example Temporal, Inngest or Restate). It holds the shared queue of work, wakes a unit at once when work arrives or on a schedule, retries after a failure, resumes interrupted work, keeps the history of every step, and can pause a unit to wait for a human approval.
3. **A shared state store** (for example a database): tasks, live state, handover notes and an append-only log of who did what.
4. **One connector, with a separate identity for each unit.** Every unit reads and writes the record and the state through it, each with its own credential and only the tools it needs. One unit can never rewrite another unit's spec or touch the stop.
5. **Units that run as code.** Each unit's instruction, tools and engine are kept in the record, and an agent runtime runs it for each job. The project in the AI assistant stays the room where humans and the unit talk.
6. **A trace of every step:** each prompt, tool call and cost, visible to the human seats.
7. **A stop outside the AI.** It revokes the unit's credential and cancels its work in the engine. Asking the AI to stop is never the stop.

**Phase one.** A small organisation may start simpler: the versioned record, a shared state store with one background worker, and the stop. It moves to the full set when its work needs it. The 49GAION text names roles only; each `[ORG]AION` names its own tools.


**A rejected proposal** goes back with its reason. Nothing of it is built, and the core proposes again.

**It never holds** another organisation's data, a credential, or a person's name.

## 4. The rules that hold

- **The branded AION builds and governs. It never runs operation.** Operation runs under Top Management. The branded AION plans it, between the units and the organisations.
- **It reads the results.** It reads the measures of success and the failures of operation, read-only, to improve the design. It gives no order in operation.
- **The builder sits outside the operation it builds.**
- **An AI proposes; a human seat decides and is accountable (HF-01).**
- **Every AIOC it installs** has a stop door, a fixed test and an accountability chain that ends at the governing seat.
- **At the handover, its Standard is identical to the 49GAION Standard or stricter.** After that it is the organisation's own, and the branded AION may change it, its principles included (section 0).

---

**Each unit the core builds** (the governing seat, 22 October 2026). In the initial standard, which the branded AION may change, each unit carries from the day it is made:
1. **A fixed test,** pass or fail, which says what the right quality is for it.
2. **A spending cap and a stop rule.**
3. **Every improvement as a new version.** It passes the fixed test before it runs, and it has a restore point.
4. **Freedom inside its own scope.** It improves itself freely there. Widening its scope, or touching another unit's work, needs a human seat's signature.

**The panels** (the governing seat, 22 October 2026). In the initial standard, each unit's panel does its own work, but every panel is made from one mother.
- **From the mother, and never rebuilt by a panel:**
  - the sign-in and identity, each person signing in as their position;
  - access by position;
  - the links to the stop door and the signature screen, which are served at the gateway; a panel only links to them and is never their route (Standard §6.1, item 6);
  - the shape of the "decision needed" screen;
  - the trace view;
  - the brand's look.
- **Free for each unit:** its own screens, reports and tools.
- **One data rule:** a panel reads and writes shared data only in the shared state store, never in a database of its own.
- **From the first day,** before any unit has its own panel, one shared read-only status page shows every unit's work from the shared state, with the link to the stop.

## 5. What holds the AI in check

Words in an instruction are not enough. In the initial standard, these hold the AI in check:

1. **Installing is a signed act.** These are listed signature types, and a human seat signs each one (Standard §4.4, item 6):
   - creating or installing a unit;
   - changing a parent;
   - moving a HOAC label;
   - approving a change, which covers a change to the Blueprint;
   - changing the organisation's Standard.
2. **The workspace holds no keys.** Every action that has an effect outside the workspace passes the tool gateway, which sits outside every AI. In phase one, while there is no gateway, a human step does each install.
3. **The stop door sits outside the core.** The governing seat can stop the core at any time, and no AI can restart it.
4. **A proposal arrives as completed staff work.** It comes as one recommendation, with its reasons, its risks, whether it can be reversed, and what waits meanwhile. Before it reaches the human seat, a part that did not write it checks it. This keeps the seat from approving what it has not been able to read.
5. **Proposals are kept to a size** the governing seat's review time can carry (section 2, item 2).

## 6. Settled in the walk-through, and what stays open

**Settled** (the governing seat, 22 October 2026, GOV):
1. **Self-Improvement is at every level.** Each unit improves its own work within its scope. At the governance level it is basic change: the structure, the Standard and the Blueprint. It is not one part that sees everything.
2. **`[ORG]AION` is a living core** that holds the organisation's design, builds from it and improves it over time; not a document that someone updates by hand.
3. **Target AI Operation sits inside the branded AION** (DR-0033). The core first writes its own target AI operation, and builds the organisation's AI operation from it.
4. **The Charter, OCO and who signs what after day one** belong to each organisation's own design. Edition Maker sets no must for them.

5. **Nothing stays fixed after the handover** (GOV, 22 October 2026), the Human Floor and human accountability included: 49GAION's authority ends where the brand is added. Its one lever is the central support line (section 0).

## 7. What was not taken from the reviews, and why

- **"Replace 'never how or by whom'"** (OpenAI). The principle stays: it is the core of AI-native (DR-0031). What the reviewer wanted protected, the existing safeguards, is now taken in as a fact (item 7).
- **"A lighter shape for small cases"** (Gemini). The shape stays the same, as the governing seat set; only the size shrinks (section 1, item 3).
- **"Return only a signed constraint sheet; no install"** (Grok). The branded AION is the builder, as the governing seat set. What the reviewer feared is held by section 5 instead: installs are signed acts, and the workspace holds no keys.
- **"A named person who is legally liable"** (Grok, OpenAI). The seat is a position. The holder is a real person, recorded in the private holders register, outside the workspace.
