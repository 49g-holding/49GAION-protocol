## grok / grok-4.7

This is a production line, not a governing seat. It says how jobs move, not what the AI must refuse, when it must stop, or who can override it.

Do not promise three working days while review time, cost, law, and engine terms are unknown. The clock can start before the only human can read the file.

"Authorises release" is not "has read it." Only contracts require a read. Other files can ship on a stamp.

Confidentiality is unenforced. No ban on logs, training, backups, or engines whose terms are still unread. No check that "keeps no files" is true. Client files may contain instructions to the AI; nothing says to ignore them.

Missing stops: mixed medical text, jobs too big to review in time, term-base edits without approval, and a meaning check by the same system that drafted.

Change: a short charter before any workflow. No client files, no client contact, no clock until the waits are cleared. Cap jobs to proven review time. Human reads every file before release. Check record proves the read and does not copy client text. Unverified retention is a hard stop.

## gemini / gemini-4-argon

What is wrong:
- Stage 1 violates confidentiality and medical rules. To flag document type, the AI must ingest the file before you check it. This exposes confidential files and prohibited medical data to the AI immediately.
- The three-day promise is reckless. With no word-count cap and only one human, a massive or multi-file order will break the deadline.
- AI self-checking is unreliable. Having the AI check its own translation in Stage 4 will miss subtle Arabic-English meaning errors. For non-contracts, human review is absent in Stage 4.

What is missing: Arabic formatting specifics (right-to-left layout breaks complex formats); dialect and jurisdiction; data deletion.

What to change: human intake first (screen for medical content, size and format before any upload to the AI); scope the turnaround by word count and editable file types; require human review of all outputs, not only contracts, until AI accuracy is proven.
